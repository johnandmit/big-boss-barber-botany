About Us

Big Boss Barber Botany is a premier barber shop located in Howick, Auckland. We are dedicated to providing exceptional grooming services, from classic precision cuts to hot towel shaves, ensuring every client walks out looking and feeling their absolute best.


Since opening our doors, we’ve built a loyal community of clients who trust our team for consistent, high-quality results. Our barbers bring decades of combined experience and a genuine passion for their craft — whether it’s a sharp skin fade, a sculpted beard, or a relaxing hot towel finish.
